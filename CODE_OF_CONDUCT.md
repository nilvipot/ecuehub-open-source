# Code of Conduct

This project's collaboration spaces must be welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of equestrian or software experience.

Be respectful, discuss evidence, accept constructive correction, and protect personal information. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Technical disagreements do not justify personal attacks. Avoid unsupported claims about identifiable riders, judges, owners or horses.

The maintainer, [nilvipot](https://github.com/nilvipot), may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, restrict participation, or report abuse to GitHub. Responses should be proportionate and consider context and recurrence. Enforcement applies to issues, pull requests and other official project spaces.

Report conduct concerns by requesting a private contact from the maintainer. Do not post sensitive evidence publicly. If a concern involves the maintainer or you cannot reach a suitable private contact, use GitHub's built-in abuse reporting options. No confidential reporting service or response deadline is guaranteed.
